# MedRisq AI implementation outcomes

- [ ] The public landing experience uses the exact MedRisq AI name and tagline, presents the headline “Healthcare Resources. Verified Intelligence. Faster Response.”, includes Find Resources and Verify Medicine actions, a labelled synthetic-demo preview, three feature explanations, an emergency coordination preview, and a transparent simulated-data/external-integrations notice.
- [ ] The application provides role-aware demo personas for hospital staff, pharmacists, emergency coordinators, administrators, and public users; sensitive workflows are represented through server-side procedures and visible role context rather than hidden controls alone.
- [ ] Resource discovery supports hospitals, pharmacies, medicines, locality/district and resource-type filters, bed/ICU/oxygen/supply views, reported quantities, source labels, freshness timestamps, reported-versus-confirmed distinction, facility details, confirmation requests, and a functional list view when mapping is unavailable.
- [ ] Medicine verification accepts manual identifier and batch entry, supports a camera-scan affordance where available and optional evidence upload, looks up deterministic synthetic registry records, compares product/manufacturer/batch/expiry/serial evidence, detects duplicate serial reuse, saves attempts, and shows Registry Match, Suspicious, Expired, or Unverified outcomes with limitations and next steps.
- [ ] Hospital and pharmacy inventory views expose quantities, units, freshness, low-stock thresholds, shortage reporting, stock history, acknowledgement actions, and validation preventing negative stock or unauthorized changes in the demo workflow.
- [ ] Trust-aware emergency matching ranks possible suppliers deterministically using resource type, available quantity, distance, freshness, reliability, commitments, and transfer status; every match explains its reasons and is revalidated before transition.
- [ ] Emergency exchange supports Request → Match → Offer → Approval → Dispatch → Receipt → Closure plus rejection, cancellation, partial fulfillment, failed-transfer states, duplicate-action prevention, valid transitions, role context, quantities, organizations, timestamps, notes, event timeline, and append-only audit events.
- [ ] Alerts cover low inventory, stale information, sudden stock changes, suspicious identifiers, conflicting records, expiring inventory, unfulfilled requests, and failed/delayed transfers; each alert has reason, source, timestamp, severity, recommended action, and acknowledgement state.
- [ ] Synthetic demo records include multiple hospitals and pharmacies, different capacities, oxygen and essential supplies, matching/suspicious/expired/unverified medicine cases, low-stock and stale alerts, and emergency requests in multiple stages; reset functionality restores the demo state.
- [ ] The project includes `public/manus-routes.json`, environment-variable/setup documentation, known limitations, and actual validation results; no secrets are exposed to the browser.

## Connected innovation upgrade

- [ ] The Innovation Lab exposes five integrated modules: AI emergency resource digital twin, Medicine Trust Passport, Predictive Emergency Resource Matching, Inter-Hospital Medicine Rescue Network, and Disaster Healthcare Swarm Intelligence.
- [ ] The digital twin displays at least five synthetic hospitals, bed/ICU/ventilator/oxygen/staffing status, hospital/regional capacity context, utilization trends, 6/12/24-hour shortage forecasts, confidence, source freshness, and scenario simulation controls; simulated predictions are clearly labelled.
- [ ] The Medicine Trust Passport displays manufacturer, batch, expiry, registry posture, supply-chain events, scan history, duplicate identifier/location/frequency/batch/expiry anomaly flags, and the limitation that only an authoritative source can support a verified label.
- [ ] Predictive emergency matching accepts capability, urgency, location, and resource needs without unnecessary patient details; ranks participating hospitals using transparent weighted factors; displays simulated map posture, capacity, travel, freshness, and availability; and supports Request Sent, Pending, Accepted, Declined, and Completed states with explicit confirmation gates.
- [ ] The Medicine Rescue Network displays surplus, quantity, batch, expiry, storage requirement, verification posture, locations, travel feasibility, and urgency; supports approval before transfer and flags cold-chain/restricted stock for additional review.
- [ ] Disaster Swarm Intelligence supports flood, earthquake, mass-casualty, and hospital-outage scenarios; changing severity recalculates demand, affected facilities, bottlenecks, oxygen/bed need, timeline, and redistribution suggestions; actions link to referral and rescue modules.
- [ ] The five modules share synthetic hospitals, medicines, alerts, and scenario state; digital-twin shortage signals can lead into rescue/referral actions; trust-passport status is visible before rescue approval; and the disaster center acts on the same facilities.

## Security-first hospital platform outcomes

- [ ] **Separate patient, approved hospital staff, and administrator portals with backend-enforced authorization:** Staff access is granted only to verified and approved hospital staff; anyone registering as staff remains pending until an authorized administrator approves them; patients can use only their own profile, appointments, records, requests, and notifications; patients cannot access staff dashboards, staff management tools, internal hospital data, or other patients’ private information; staff cannot access administrative functions unless separately authorized; direct protected URL/API requests are rejected with appropriate authorization errors; users cannot change their own role or approve their own staff account.
- [ ] **Persistent staff application approval workflow:** Authorized administrators can view pending applications and transition staff applications between pending, approved, rejected, and suspended states; public registration cannot create administrator accounts; approval, rejection, suspension, login, logout, and denied-access events are recorded in append-only audit records.
- [ ] **Functional patient portal:** Authenticated users can create or update a synthetic patient profile, book/view/cancel appointments, submit hospital service requests, view request statuses and notifications, and retain data across refresh and logout/login; all patient reads and writes are ownership-scoped on the backend.
- [ ] **Functional approved-staff portal:** Approved staff can view only the hospital workflows allowed by their organization membership and permission set, manage assigned appointment/request status, communicate through request updates, and cannot access another organization’s private patient data; pending, rejected, or suspended staff are blocked.
- [ ] **Security and deployment documentation with tests:** Add server-side validation, protected procedures, session/logout handling, secure role transitions, database setup notes, synthetic-data warnings, and automated tests covering patient access, pending/rejected/suspended staff blocking, approved staff access, admin-only approval, IDOR prevention, appointment persistence, request status updates, and unauthorized direct access.
